    Enumeration (GI Flags)

    NM-1.0 ▶ NM ▶ DeviceManagedFlagsSince 1.58

    Flags for the SetManaged() D-Bus call of a device and nm_device_set_managed_async().

    NM.DeviceManagedFlags.PERMANENT_BY_NAME and NM.DeviceManagedFlags.PERMANENT_BY_MAC are mutually exclusive, and they only make sense together with NM.DeviceManagedFlags.PERMANENT. If none is set, the matching criteria is selected automatically.
